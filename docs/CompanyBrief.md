# Southeast Code

Southeast Code builds custom web applications for organizations that have outgrown spreadsheets, disconnected systems, and legacy internal software.

Based near Savannah, Georgia, we turn fragmented, manual workflows into centralized, secure operational software built around how your organization works and where its processes need to improve.

## What We Do

We design and develop custom software that maps to your specific business logic:

- **Operational Dashboards & Portals:** Centralized hubs for managing active workflows, daily operations, approvals, and core business data.
- **Internal Tools & Admin Systems:** Purpose-built applications designed to reduce repetitive manual work and prevent common errors.
- **System Integration:** Connecting isolated software, field data, and third-party APIs to create a more consistent and reliable view of operational information.
- **Reporting & Data Visibility:** Clear, accessible reporting that gives teams and decision-makers timely operational insight.
- **Legacy Modernization:** Rebuilding unstable or difficult-to-maintain internal systems as fast, secure, mobile-friendly web applications.
- **Granular Access Control:** Role- and permission-based architecture tailored for secure internal and client-facing use.
- **Progressive Web Applications (PWAs):** Responsive applications that provide app-like functionality across desktop and mobile devices without requiring traditional app store distribution.

## Who We Help

We partner with small to mid-sized organizations--particularly in operations-heavy sectors such as logistics, fulfillment, and light manufacturing--where daily execution depends on accurate, repeatable data.

### Our typical client:

- Loses critical hours to manual data entry, spreadsheet chains, and fragmented workarounds.
- Struggles with data silos and disconnected tools that do not communicate reliably.
- Needs high-utility operational software rather than another standard marketing website.
- Has outgrown an aging internal application or off-the-shelf SaaS platform.
- Requires a dependable, long-term technical partner rather than a transactional, one-off agency.

## How We Work

We map your physical and digital workflows before writing code. Technology is selected only after the underlying business needs, bottlenecks, and movement of data are clearly understood.

We preserve the processes that work, improve the ones that do not, and avoid forcing your business into an abstract technical model.

### Our development standards emphasize:

- **Operational Alignment:** Software engineered around established workflows while minimizing team friction during rollout.
- **Clear Architecture:** Performant, maintainable, and secure code without unnecessary technical complexity.
- **Accessible Interfaces:** Intuitive, focused software designed for the people who use it every day.
- **Reliable Infrastructure:** Stable deployment, dependable background processing, secure data storage, and appropriate operational monitoring.
- **Long-Term Extensibility:** Software designed to avoid unnecessary vendor lock-in and evolve alongside your requirements and transaction volume.
- **Direct Partnership:** Straightforward communication, transparent technical milestones, and explanations without unnecessary corporate or technical jargon.

## Technology

Our core technical stack includes **PHP, Laravel, Vue.js, TypeScript, Inertia.js, MySQL, Redis, and Tailwind CSS**.

We favor proven, scalable open-source technologies that support long-term maintainability, flexibility, and client ownership. This approach reduces unnecessary vendor lock-in and recurring platform costs while allowing each system to evolve with the organization.
